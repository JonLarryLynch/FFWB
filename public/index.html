The race

League position after each scored week. The top three as it stands are in colour. Hover a line to follow one player.

Title odds

A simulation, strictly for fun. The rest of the season is replayed thousands of times using past league weeks, starting from today's points. First half is the chance of topping the table at the halfway mark, and Title is the chance of finishing the season on top.

Where the picks went

Every pick the league has made on a club, split by how it landed. Per pick counts a win as 1 and a draw as a half. Club per game is what the club itself has averaged, so a gap between the two is timing.

Picks used this half

This season's bragging rights. They are worked out again every time a week is scored, and a tie shares the award.

Sheep and mavericks

How much of the league was on the same club each time a player made a pick, averaged over the season. A solo pick is a club nobody else took that week.

Can you beat the Herd?

The Herd is a phantom sixteenth player. Each week it locks whatever most of the league locked and takes the four other most popular clubs. Finish below it and you would have done better copying everyone else.

What the Herd picked

Pick a name for their card. Career figures cover completed seasons. The two rival figures are the share of weeks they outscore that player, leaving out level weeks.

Career records

Completed seasons only, with titles and finishes on full-season points. Pts vs league avg is a player's average gap to that season's league average, which keeps high and low scoring years comparable.

Finishing position by season

Full-season finish, in career order. Filled cells are titles and darker shading is a higher finish.

Honours

As recorded on the Trophies & Statistics tab. Blank cells are blank there too.

Clubs, all-time

Every scored pick since 2016-17, for clubs picked at least 100 times. Locks won shows once a club has 20 locks.

The 2025-26 points come from the 2026 Table tab. The entries tab for that season is only scored through its first weeks, so its later picks are left out of the club and lock figures.

Trophy cabinet

Every honour on record. League titles are counted from full-season points and the rest from the Trophies & Statistics tab. A shared cup counts for both names.

Heart or head

Picks on the club beside each player's name. Times as often compares how much they pick it with how much everyone else does. Edge is their points per pick on it against the rest of the league's.

Big weeks and lock runs

A perfect week is every pick winning. Weeks on top are weeks with the highest score or a share of it. A shocker is a week of 1.5 points or fewer. Lock runs carry across seasons.

Black weekends

Every week when not one lock in the league won, and the club most of you had locked when it happened.

Lucky clubs and bogey clubs

The club where each player most beats the rest of the league, and the one where they most trail it, on points per pick in the same seasons. Only clubs they have picked at least 40 times.

Head to head

Read across: the share of weeks the player on the left outscored the player above, leaving out level weeks. Players are in order of their overall record.
