Set the cup weeks

League Cup in the autumn, Festive Fixtures at Christmas, FA Cup in the spring. Choose each cup’s weeks and save them. That is all: each cup draws itself when its first week starts, seeded from the table at that point, and everyone in it gets an alert. Knockouts run one round a week, with the top of the table getting a bye. Festive Fixtures is the most points over any weeks you choose, in a row or not. Each week can only belong to one cup.

Off means people can keep picking until you close the week or enter a result, like the sheet. With it on, click a week that has kicked off to reopen it for someone late. EFL picks

Switch the EFL pick on for any week. Players then get one optional Championship pick that week, worth 1 for a win and a half for a draw, never doubled, and added to their season total.

Personal links

Text each player their own link. Opening it links that phone or laptop to their name, and from then on only their linked devices can change their picks or see them before kickoff. They can open the same link on as many of their own devices as they like. Add a mobile number or email under Contact and the Text and Email buttons fill in who it goes to. Until someone opens theirs, they can still pick the old way. Worried a link got passed around? Reset it, and only the new one works.

Players

Rename someone, add a new player, or retire someone who has left. A retired player drops off this season's table and can be brought back any time. Past seasons keep the names they were played under.

The Shame Club

Anyone whose picks include both teams from the same match joins automatically. This list is for members from before the site, one per line. Put a title in brackets and a nickname after aka, like Greg (Platinum Member) or Michael aka Diddler.

Site errors

If the site breaks on someone's phone, it sends a note here: what went wrong, where, who was using it and on what. The same problem is counted rather than listed twice.

Backup

The site saves a backup every Tuesday morning and keeps the last 12. You can also download everything (picks, results and the change log) as one file right now.

Change log

Every saved change to picks or results, newest first. If someone changes another player's picks, it shows here.
