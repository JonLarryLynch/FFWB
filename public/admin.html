This page is for entering results, opening and closing weeks, and fixing anyone's picks.

Results

Set each club's result for the week: W, D, L or P for postponed. A postponed game scores nothing until you come back and give it a result. As soon as any result is saved, that week closes to picks. Once all 20 clubs have one, the next week opens.

Phone alert

Players turn alerts on from the Picks tab. The site sends them automatically when a week opens, and reminds anyone who has not picked about a day and three hours before kickoff.

Weeks

Players can pick the current week and any later week. Earlier weeks, and any week with results in, are closed.

Week

Close a week early

Off means people can keep picking until you close the week or enter a result, like the sheet. With it on, click a week that has kicked off to reopen it for someone late. Click any week to close it early, and again to undo. Orange is closed by you, grey has kicked off, outlined is reopened by you, green has results in.

EFL picks

Switch the EFL pick on for any week. Players then get one optional Championship pick that week, worth 1 for a win and a half for a draw, never doubled, and added to their season total.

Backup

The site saves a backup every Tuesday morning and keeps the last 12. You can also download everything (picks, results and the change log) as one file right now.

Fix anyone's picks

Any player, any week, including closed weeks. If picks break a rule you will see why, and can save them anyway.

Change log

Every saved change to picks or results, newest first. If someone changes another player's picks, it shows here.
