This page is for entering results, opening and closing weeks, and fixing anyone's picks.

Results

Set each club's result for the week: W, D, L or P for postponed. A postponed game scores nothing until you come back and give it a result. As soon as any result is saved, that week closes to picks. Once all 20 clubs have one, the next week opens.

Week

Weeks

Players can pick the current week and any later week. Earlier weeks, and any week with results in, are closed.

Week

Close a week early

Click a week to close it to picks, say at the first kickoff, and click again to reopen it. Filled orange means closed by you, green means results are in.

Backup

Download everything (picks, results and the change log) as one file to keep somewhere safe.

Anyone's picks

Fix or fill in picks for any player and any week, including closed weeks. If picks break a rule you will see why, and you can choose to save them anyway.

Change log

Every saved change to picks or results, newest first. Players do not sign in, so if someone changes another player's picks, it shows here.
